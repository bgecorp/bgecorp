Started online, built on practical products

BGE Corporation opened on May 18, 2018 in Central, Quezon City, as an e-commerce company. The work was simple to describe: find useful products made abroad, import them, and offer them through e-commerce.

Our first line was home medical devices. Blood pressure monitors, thermometers and pulse oximeters are small tools, but they let a household keep an eye on its health between visits to the doctor. They gave the company its start and remain a core part of what we bring in.

From there we added modern kitchenware, including multicookers and vegetable multi-slicers, for people who want to prepare meals with less time and effort.

We also import and distribute beverages, with barley and coffee as our main drinks.

Our imports now extend to digital machines for business as well: point-of-sale (POS) machines that stores use to record sales at the counter.

More recently, we have ventured into digital products and services. We specialize in web and app development, and our latest product is AIBOOM POS. Today the company works on two fronts: e-commerce, with imported products for homes and businesses, and building software.

Our latest product, AI-powered

An AI-powered POS to help your business boom.

AIBOOM POS is a point-of-sale system developed by BGE Corporation. It handles selling at the counter, keeps track of products and stock, and shows how the store is doing each day. It runs on a phone, a tablet or a POS terminal.

  • Selling Point of sale and sales history
  • Inventory Products, categories and barcodes
  • Restocking Stock predictor and shopping list
  • Utang Customer credit and recorded payments
  • Reports Daily sales, profit and best sellers
  • AI Advisor Ask about sales, inventory or unpaid utang

Ask the built-in AI Advisor

Store owners can ask it about their sales, inventory or unpaid utang. Example questions:

  • How are my sales today?
  • Which items are running low?
  • Who has unpaid utang?
